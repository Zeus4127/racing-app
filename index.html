Get the app

Track Edge on your home screenFree, no store, no sign-up. It is this website, so it always has the newest version and works offline too.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap Install app above. If nothing pops up, tap the ⋮ menu at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then tap Install.
  3. Open Track Edge from your home screen.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Track Edge is now on your home screen.
