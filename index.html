The honest part

Winning often is easy. Winning money is not.

Backing favourites wins a lot of races and still loses money, because the odds are too short. What matters is whether a dog's real chance is bigger than the chance built into its price. This site works out each runner's chance from past results, turns it into a fair price, and shows how well that held up on races it had never seen.
